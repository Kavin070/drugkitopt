# Tropical environment

Tropical rainforest environments can involve high humidity, heat stress, standing water, insect exposure, fungal skin problems, and sanitation-related infectious disease risks. Field medical planning should consider hydration, heat illness prevention, insect-bite prevention, skin care, wound hygiene, and access to medical evacuation.

For an AI system, regional context should be treated as a risk signal rather than a diagnosis. The system should not infer that a person has a specific infection merely because they are operating in a tropical region.
